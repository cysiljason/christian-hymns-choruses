Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Echoes of Praise ("the app") is developed and published by an individual developer. This policy explains what data the app does — and does not — collect.

Summary

The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. It works offline and has no user accounts, no advertising, and no analytics. The only exception is the optional voice search described below, which uses your phone's built-in speech service.

Data stored on your device

The app saves a few preferences locally on your own device only, using standard Android/iOS app storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer or any third party:

  • Songs you mark as favorites
  • Playlists you create
  • Your chosen text size
  • Your chosen theme (light/dark/system)
  • Your chosen song language

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data.

Voice search

The Search screen has an optional microphone button. Voice search only runs when you tap it, and the app asks for permission to use your microphone and speech recognition the first time you do.

When you use it, your phone's own speech recognition service (Apple's on iPhone and iPad, Google's or your device maker's on Android) turns what you say into text. To do this, that service may send the recording to Apple or Google, under their privacy policies: Apple Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy.

The app only receives the resulting text, which it uses to search the songs on your device. The app does not record, store, or send your voice or the search text anywhere, and the developer never receives them.

Third-party services

The app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in the future (for example, if a new feature requires data collection), this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
