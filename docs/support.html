Frequently Asked Questions

Does the app need an internet connection?
No. All songs are stored on your device, so the app works fully offline at all times.

How do I switch languages?
Tap the language (globe) icon on the Home screen, or open Settings and change the Language option.

How do I search for a song?
Use the Search tab and type a song number, title, or a line from the lyrics.

How do I save favorites or make a playlist?
Tap the heart icon on any song to favorite it. Go to the Favorites tab and select the Playlists tab to create and manage custom playlists.

How do I change the text size or theme?
Open the Settings tab to adjust the lyrics font size or switch between light, dark, or system theme.

Does the app collect any personal data?
No. See the Privacy Policy for details.
